How it works

Three steps to
agentic spend.

01
Define your budget tree
Map your annual budget into the hierarchy that matches your org: company → dept → team → agent. Assign a controller at each level with their own approval authority.
02
Register your agents
Give each AI agent an identity, a wallet, and a policy — max transaction, allowed categories, auto-approve ceiling, escalation rules. Done via API or dashboard in minutes.
03
Let agents run. Earn cashback.
Agents spend within policy autonomously. Anything above threshold pings the right human. Every transaction earns 1.5% cashback routed back to your team wallet.
